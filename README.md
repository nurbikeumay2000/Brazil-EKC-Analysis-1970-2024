# Environmental Kuznets Curve (EKC) Analysis for Brazil (1970–2024)

## 1. Introduction

This project examines the Environmental Kuznets Curve (EKC) hypothesis for Brazil over the period 1970–2024. The main purpose is to investigate the relationship between economic growth and CO2 emissions per capita and to determine whether environmental pollution decreases as income increases.

## 2. Data and Variables

The study uses annual data for Brazil from 1970 to 2024. The data were obtained from the World Bank's World Development Indicators (WDI) database.

The variables used in the analysis are:

- **LNCO2:** Natural logarithm of CO2 emissions per capita.
- **LNGDPPC:** Natural logarithm of real GDP per capita (constant 2015 US$).
- **LNGDPPC²:** Squared term of the natural logarithm of real GDP per capita.

## 3. Methodology

A quadratic regression model is estimated using the Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) method. The model examines whether there is an inverted-U-shaped relationship between GDP per capita and CO2 emissions per capita.

## 4. Main Findings

The regression results show a negative coefficient for the linear GDP term and a positive coefficient for its squared term. These results suggest a U-shaped relationship rather than the inverted-U shape predicted by the EKC hypothesis.

The estimated results do not provide strong evidence supporting the EKC hypothesis for Brazil during the period studied.
